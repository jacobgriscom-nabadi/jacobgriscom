# My Political Journey

Waking up to tribal blindness after the 2016 election — and the search for a way of seeing that holds Red, Blue, and Modern America without collapsing into any one.

I suspect that the capacity to include perspectives — without dissolving into relativism or domination — is the only force capable of counterbalancing a looming period of American fragmentation.

## The Wake-Up



Waking up on November 9, 2016, to the confirmation that Donald J. Trump had been elected the 45th president of the United States marked a clear inflection point for me.


Raised inside a deeply Blue California bubble — under the intensely Blue tribal perspective of my mother and her side of the family — I had assumed Hillary Clinton's victory was inevitable. Like many others, I took this outcome for granted.


But where others felt destabilized or horrified, I became intensely curious. It was immediately obvious that I was missing something fundamental. I was disconnected from roughly half of my countrymen and had no real understanding of their values, priorities, histories, or fears. I had been taught — implicitly and explicitly — that Red America was some combination of ignorant or immoral, and that our positions were self-evidently correct and evolved. Any educated person would see this. Of course.


## The Integral Lens



My earlier exposure to Integral philosophy had already introduced a destabilizing idea: that each stage of development is necessary, builds on what came before it, and creates the conditions for what comes after. Complexity increases. New capacities emerge, along with new ways to fail. Problems arise when an individual or culture attempts to transcend earlier stages without including them. What gets rejected does not disappear — it returns as projection, hostility, and enemy image.


## Reshaping the Diet



In response, I deliberately re-shaped my information diet. I began actively including Red America's traditionalism and nationalism, and Modern America's capitalism and technologism, to counterbalance the overexposed mono-diet of Blue America's post-modernism.


At a high level:


**Red America** is concerned with continuity — tradition, family, place, and social cohesion.


**Blue America** focuses on the constraints and injustices imposed by inherited structures.


**Modern America** pushes toward the frontier — growth, innovation, efficiency, and control of the future.


Each carries essential truth. Each also collapses into predictable failure modes.


## Failure Modes



### Red America — Closure



Becomes defensive rather than discerning. Tradition hardens into identity. Novel ideas are treated as threats instead of probes. Experimentation is rejected in favor of preservation. Strength collapses into stagnation.


### Blue America — Moral Reduction



Interprets reality through a single moral lens. Complexity is flattened into oppressed versus oppressor. Inquiry is replaced by accusation. Disagreement becomes evidence of harm. Truth is subordinated to moral signaling.


### Modern America — Control Drift



Trades meaning for management. Replaces trust with systems, metrics, and enforcement. Optimizes behavior while neglecting character. Stability is purchased at the cost of aliveness. A technically efficient society hollows out its soul.


## Reintegration



I explored these perspectives not as abstract ideologies or political affiliations, but through rediscovery of occluded territory within myself. As those fragments reintegrated, my speech, priorities, and lines of inquiry necessarily shifted. That shift created friction for anyone whose identity depended on a fixed tribal orientation. I discovered that this category includes most people.


I also came to understand that even entertaining such exploration carries risk. It threatens one's sense of belonging within family systems, professional networks, and financial ecosystems. From there, frustration gives way to compassion. Of course people cling. Of course they defend. I, too, wish to belong.


## Resistance



Each domain resists integration in its own way.


For Red, movement is interpreted as betrayal. For Blue, deviation is moralized and condemned. For Modern, perspective itself becomes a variable to be managed rather than inhabited.
What is needed here, now, at this scale? What mode is appropriate to this domain? What synthesis can hold overlapping truth without collapsing into tribal allegiance?

## Situational Truth



Rather than collapsing into a single frame that claims to be "most right," I am now interested in how these perspectives — and the tools and ideologies they generate — can be applied situationally, like elemental qualities in Ayurveda or the balance of Yin and Yang.


What is needed here, now, at this scale? What mode is appropriate to this domain? What synthesis can hold overlapping truth without collapsing into tribal allegiance? How do subjective, objective, inter-subjective, and inter-objective realities relate and inform one another?


These questions are not safe. But they open a far more interesting and generative way of seeing. I suspect that the capacity to include perspectives — without dissolving into relativism or domination — is the only force capable of counterbalancing a looming period of American fragmentation.


This is what I seek to represent. And I am looking for fellow travelers willing to walk without a single flag.
